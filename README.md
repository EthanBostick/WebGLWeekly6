### Reflection Questions

- Which lighting setup created the strongest mood?
  - probably the cozy evening. The lampshade and its light were very aesthetic. It would have been even better if the sky was darker and the lights dimmed more
- Which scene felt most realistic?
  - once again probably the cozy evening. It had the least evident lighting "artifacts", and had good vibes
- How did lighting change the emotional feeling of the room?
  - it was probably the lighting color that changed the emotional feel the most, just straight dimming the lights gave more cozy vibes depending on the color warmth
- Why are PointLights useful indoors?
  - they mimic real world indoor light sources like light bulbs and candles really well
- Why are colored lights effective for storytelling?
  - colored lights give a lot of emotional/atmospheric emphasis; the color of a light can easily change the vibes of a scene
- Which lighting setup required the most experimentation?
  - the boss lighting; it required the most effort getting the lights to work just the way I wanted.
- How did animated lights improve the scene?
  - they made the scenes feel less flat and more alive
- If this were a real game, which scene would players remember most and why?
  - probably the boss scene, because it is the most unreal scenario compared to the others. The movie night one could be a close second because its spooky
